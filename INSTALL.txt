DriveSweep — removes macOS junk (.DS_Store, ._ files, .Spotlight-V100, .fseventsd,
.Trashes, ...) from SD cards and external drives.

INSTALL
1. Drag DriveSweep into the Applications folder.
2. Open Applications, right-click DriveSweep, choose Open, then Open again.
   (macOS asks because this app isn't from the App Store.)
   If there's no Open button: System Settings > Privacy & Security >
   scroll down > "Open Anyway" next to DriveSweep.
3. A drive icon appears in the menu bar.
4. Click it > "Set Up Spotlight Helper...". In System Settings > Login Items,
   switch DriveSweep on under "Allow in the Background".
   The menu should now say "Spotlight Helper: On".

USE
- Eject a drive normally: it is cleaned first.
- Or right-click a drive in Finder > Services > Clean Junk Files.
- Or use the menu bar icon: Clean / Clean & Eject.

Only external drives are touched. Never your main disk, Time Machine, or network drives.
Note: deleting ._ files also removes Finder tags/labels on files on that drive.
